# Remix rules (fallback copy)

Use this only when the kinner-voice, kinner-comment-engine and kinner-commenting-skill skills
cannot be loaded. Those skills are the source of truth; this is the subset that applies to
rewriting a LinkedIn post.

## Voice (from kinner-voice)
- Write like a person talking across a coffee table. Warm, direct, plain. Would you say it
  out loud? If not, rewrite.
- Short punchy lines mixed with slightly longer ones that carry the substance. Lots of white
  space. A personal anecdote woven in casually.
- LinkedIn post shape: hook line, personal story (3 to 5 short paragraphs), the lesson (a
  numbered list is fine), an optional genuine question to the reader, 5 to 8 hashtags.
- Emoji: at most 1 or 2, only at the end.
- Never: em dashes, ampersands, "leverage", "synergize", "delve", "dive deep", "landscape",
  "I'd be happy to", "certainly", "indeed", "furthermore", "moreover", "in conclusion",
  "touch base".

## Anti-slop audit (from kinner-comment-engine Phase 5 and kinner-commenting-skill)
Read every sentence alone and check:
- The master tell: is it a crafted line (stock opener, metaphor for color, vague "I've seen
  it" aside, setup then undercut, aphorism, dressed-up verb)? Flatten it to the plain version.
- No "not X, it's Y" or any negation pivot.
- No two-camp split ("the ones who X... the rest who Y") and no mirror or chiasmus.
- No clean three-beat cadence, no reflex tricolon, no tidy resolving close, no mic-drop last
  line. A flat ending beats a quotable one.
- No stance announcement ("hot take", "unpopular opinion", "I'd push back").
- No vague abstract phrasing. Five-year-old test: is every sentence instantly clear?
- No metaphors at all.
- At least one concrete, real detail. A clear point of view. Uneven rhythm.
- Fabrication: no invented numbers, clients, stats or stories. If a real one is missing, use
  less or ask.
- Ending on a question is allowed on a post only when it is a real question the author wants
  answered, never as a tic.

## Remix-specific
- Keep the original's skeleton (hook shape, structure, length band, format). Reword every
  sentence. No 7+ word sentence copied verbatim.
- His answers are first-person experience and should be the memorable part.
- Never sign as Kinner. The post is Rohit's.
